# 1.0.0

First release of Know Your Rotation.

A rotation helper for WoW Forever. It shows the next three spells on a small HUD and highlights the matching action-bar button. It never casts for you and never reads the combat log.

- English only, WoW Forever (camelot client) only.
- Highlight uses the client's own spell-alert glow, and only for a spell you can press right now.
- All artwork is built-in client art; no texture files ship with the addon.
- Profiles with import / export, per-spell hold times, Single / AoE / Burst / Auto modes.
