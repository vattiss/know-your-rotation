# 1.0.0-beta.1

First beta of Know Your Rotation.

This is a beta: the addon works, but not every class and level range has been played through yet, and the default spell lists are still being tuned for WoW Forever. Please report anything odd (a wrong suggestion, a missing glow, a Lua error) on GitHub: https://github.com/attilavarga96/know-your-rotation/issues

A rotation helper for WoW Forever, made for learning a class from level 1: instead of digging through guides while you level, follow the addon. The spell to press next lights up on your action bars. There is no extra window to watch.

## What it shows
- The next rotation spell glows on its action-bar button, only when you can actually press it.
- Heals light up once your missing health is about the size of the heal, also in combat when the client hides health values.
- Defense buffs, interrupts, purges, cleanses and weapon buffs (imbues, poisons, stones) each get their own glow, with an optional sound.
- A screen message when a cue spell is not on your action bars, and pet reminders for hunters and warlocks.

## What it does on its own
- Role from your talents, stance and the distance to your target.
- Single-target or AoE list from the number of nearby enemies.
- PvE or PvP profile from the instance type, your PvP flag or an enemy player as target.
- Each of these can be fixed by hand on the Behavior page.

## Options
- Game menu → Options → AddOns → Know Your Rotation, built from the game's own settings controls.
- Highlights: per-cue switch, sound and color, and a switch to disable the addon on one character.
- Behavior: role, combat mode, AoE threshold, profile, smart heal threshold, action-bar-only suggestions, Classic swing and auto-shot timing.
- Rotation and Defense: edit one list at a time, drag spells in from the spellbook, optional per-spell delay.
- Profiles: reset, export and import.

## Scope
- WoW Forever (camelot client) only, English only.
- Tuned for leveling and learning: no burst or raid modes.
- No chat commands, no minimap button, no texture files; all artwork is built into the client.
- It never reads the combat log.
