# 1.0.0-beta.1

First beta of Know Your Rotation.

This is a beta: the addon works, but not every class and level range has been played through yet, and the default spell lists are still being tuned for WoW Forever. Please report anything odd (a wrong suggestion, a missing glow, a Lua error) on GitHub: https://github.com/vattiss/know-your-rotation/issues

A rotation helper for WoW Forever, made for learning a class from level 1: instead of digging through guides while you level, follow the addon. The spell to press next lights up on your action bars. There is no extra window to watch.

## What it shows
- The next rotation spell glows on its action-bar button, only when you can actually press it.
- Heals light up once your missing health is about the size of the heal, also in combat when the client hides health values. Heals and shields also work on a party or raid member you select: quick heals once enough health is missing, Renew / Power Word: Shield whenever they are missing.
- Defense buffs, interrupts, purges, cleanses and weapon buffs (imbues, poisons on each hand, stones) each get their own glow, with an optional sound.
- Shaman totems and the hunter Auto Shot / Concussive Shot get their own cue next to the rotation, so the other buttons keep going.
- The pull: an offensive racial first, then a cast-time attack while the mob runs in, then the instants.
- Screen messages when the next spells are learned but on no action bar (the glow moves on to the next one), when a bar holds an older rank than the one you know, and pet reminders for hunters and warlocks.
- Quiet in towns and inns: no glow or message while resting out of combat (can be switched off).

## What it does on its own
- Role from the name of your main talent tree (Protection = tank; Holy, Discipline, Restoration = heal), and melee or ranged from the distance to your target.
- Single-target or AoE list from the number of nearby enemies.
- PvE or PvP profile from the instance type, your PvP flag or an enemy player as target.
- Each of these can be fixed by hand on the Behavior page.

## Options
- Game menu → Options → AddOns → Know Your Rotation, built from the game's own settings controls.
- Highlights: per-cue switch, sound and color, a switch to disable the addon on one character, and the quiet-in-towns switch.
- Behavior: role, combat mode, AoE threshold, profile, smart heal threshold, Classic swing and auto-shot timing, debug data collection.
- Rotation and Defense: edit one list at a time, drag spells in from the spellbook, optional per-spell delay.
- Profiles: reset, export and import.

## Tested so far
- Played to about level 10 on shaman, rogue, hunter, priest and druid. Warrior, mage, warlock and paladin are built from the spell data and the API docs, not played yet.
- Levels above 20 have not been played: the lists for them follow the spell data. Reports from those levels help the most.
- To help: switch on Behavior > Collect debug data, play until the problem happens, then press Debug report on the Profiles page and paste the text into your issue (the README says more).

## Scope
- WoW Forever (camelot client) only, English only.
- Tuned for leveling and learning: no burst or raid modes.
- No chat commands and no minimap button. The add-on list icon is the only artwork of its own; every glow and icon in the game is built into the client.
- It never reads the combat log.
